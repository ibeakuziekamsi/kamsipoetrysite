Alone, But Not Lonely

Silence

She's mad at me, They're mad at me, But I'm just me, And I can't help it. I don't fit into the environment They want me in. I love my space, Quietly keeping up my pace. With so many things to worry about, I can't afford to stress over frivolities. So many rocks to break, Why stumble over stones? Only I understand myself, Just wishing someone could lend me a hand. For now, I cast regret aside And turn it into a reason to advance. To make sure I laugh at my struggles, Not cry over them. I love being alone, but not lonely. I value my privacy, Keeping to my policy Of not showing my weakness publicly. I have my regrets, yeah, But they can't keep me locked in. I have to step over them, I have to look beyond them. Of course, I mask it with my smile, Struggling to hold back my tears, Trying to bury my fears. I just wish they could understand. I wish you could too, and I hope you will. --- Ibeakuzie Kamsiriochukwu
